1) ENTERPRISE INFORMATON SYSTEMS MAPPING

TRANSACTION PROCESSING SYSTEM(TPS)
-SuperMart SA uses the transaction processing system to record the daily transactions for example what customers purchases, the payments, the refunds and their daily recieved stock at the warehouse, and also updates inventory when products are sold or new stock is recieved, so this helps the company to know that any transactions are recorded correctly without mistakes.

MANAGEMENT INFORMATION SYSTEM(MIS) AND DECISION SUPPORT SYSTEMS (DDS)
-the MIS helps managers track their weekly sales, the perfomance of their employees, stock level(is it over(more) or average).A DDS also helps managers to make decisions by looking at sales data and identifying valid reasons for a loss or a drop in sales, so it can also help them to make decisions for instance whether to run a promotion while comparing costs, the customers needs and wants and the expected profits

EXECUTIVE SUPPORT SYSTEM(ESS)
- The owner uses the ESS to look the high-level reports about total profits from the company, the sales perfomance, the marketing trends, and their competitors activities so this information helps the owner to analyse and make valuable decisions for the future

KNOWLEDGE WORK SYSTEMS(KWS) AND OFFICE AUTOMATION SYSTEMS(OAS)
-The example of KWS is computer-aided design(CAD) software it is usually used by the designers to help them create the company or store layouts and the stores product displays.
-An OAS example is Microsoft Excel so many employess use it to manage their schedules, prepare their reports or presentations and organise daily administrative information

  SuperMart SA can identify long or outdated hardware and software that are also expensive to maintain or the company no longer receive the security updates, so replacing or removing these systems at the right time will help the company to reduce maintainance costs and the companys security risks

-Standardization
 SuperMart SA can use common software, hardware and the technology around its departments, and this helps the company to spend less money on training, simplifies maintanance and helps emmployees to share more information between various departments
